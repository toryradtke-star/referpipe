# How to judge a referral partner

A good partner is a local, independently run business whose clients regularly end up with injuries or pain, and who
would plausibly send them to an outside PT.

- **Fit** (0–100): highest for strength and CrossFit gyms, trainers, running stores, youth sports associations, and
  sports-focused chiropractors and massage therapists; middle for general gyms and yoga studios; low otherwise.
- **Tier**: `hot` = clear fit and a published email; `warm` = real fit, phone or form only; `cold` = thin fit;
  `skip` = a competitor (anything offering physical therapy or rehab itself), a hospital or health system, a chain,
  or outside the area.
- Never assume. If their site doesn't say they work with athletes or injuries, don't claim it.
